What's on the shelf

Everything below is sold loose, by weight, from our dispensers and jars — all of it vegan friendly and plant based. Stock moves with the seasons and with what's good, so if you're after something particular, ring ahead or ask when you're in.

Prices

Everything is priced per 100g (or per 100ml for liquids) and the price is on the dispenser. There's no minimum — a spoonful of chia costs a spoonful. We'd rather you took a little and came back than bought a bag you don't finish.

Nuts & dried fruit

Almonds, cashews, walnuts, brazils, hazelnuts, peanuts, pecans, pistachios. Dates, apricots, figs, mango, pineapple, cranberries, raisins, sultanas, mixed peel.

Grains, pasta & legumes

Spaghetti, penne, fusilli, lasagne sheets. Red and green lentils, chickpeas, kidney beans, black beans, butter beans. Basmati, brown rice, arborio, oats, quinoa, couscous, bulgur, pearl barley.

Seeds, snacks & baking

Pumpkin, sunflower, chia, flax, sesame, hemp. Trail mix, banana chips, coated peanuts, pretzels. Flours, sugars, cocoa, desiccated coconut, baking powder, herbs and spices.

Cleaning refills

Plant-based and cruelty-free. Washing-up liquid, laundry liquid, fabric conditioner, multi-surface spray, bathroom cleaner, floor cleaner, hand soap, white vinegar, bicarbonate of soda.

Toiletries

Shampoo, conditioner, body wash and hand wash on refill. Soap and shampoo bars, bamboo toothbrushes, toothpaste tabs, deodorant, reusable cotton pads and other plastic-free swaps.

Seasonal goodies

Festive mixes, Easter treats, summer picnic bits — whatever the calendar calls for. Follow @ecorefill_liverpool to see what's just landed.

Local & handmade

Not everything here comes out of a dispenser. We keep a shelf of handmade artisan goods from makers round here — small batches, made locally, the sort of thing that makes a good gift. What's on it changes as the makers do, so it's worth a look every visit.

Make something yourself and think it belongs on that shelf? Email us or send a message on Instagram.
