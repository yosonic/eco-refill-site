What's on the shelf

Everything below is sold loose, by weight, from our dispensers and jars — and more than 95% of it is organic and vegan. Stock moves with the seasons and with what's good, so if you're after something particular, ring ahead or ask when you're in.

Prices

Everything is priced per 100g, or per 100ml for liquids, and herbs and spices per 10g. The price is on the dispenser. There's no minimum — a spoonful of chia costs a spoonful. We'd rather you took a little and came back than bought a bag you don't finish.

Nuts & dried fruit

Almonds whole, flaked and ground, cashews, hazelnuts, pecans, walnut halves, brazils, pine nuts, peanuts, roasted and salted pistachios, mixed nuts. Pitted and chopped dates, apricots, figs, prunes, mango strips, cranberries, white mulberries, raisins, sultanas, banana chips, coconut flakes and desiccated coconut.

Grains, pasta & pulses

White and wholewheat spaghetti, penne, pennette, fusilli, orzo, mini shells, tricolour pasta, gluten-free casarecce, red lentil elbows, wheat noodles and ramen kits. Red, green, brown and puy lentils, chickpeas, kidney, pinto, aduki and black turtle beans, yellow split peas. Basmati white and brown, arborio, jasmine, sushi, black and short grain brown rice, tricolour quinoa, couscous and giant couscous, pearl barley, popping corn.

Cereals, seeds & baking

Jumbo and porridge oats, deluxe muesli, granola base, bran flakes, puffed quinoa. Pumpkin, sunflower, chia, golden linseed, sesame and black sesame, hulled hemp, poppy and omega mix seeds. Raw cane, light granulated, demerara, muscovado and coconut sugar, cocoa powder, raw cacao nibs, arrowroot, bicarbonate of soda, engevita flakes, crystallised ginger.

Herbs, spices & tea

Herbs and spices by the 10g, so you buy a recipe's worth and no more. Basil, oregano, parsley, rosemary, sage, thyme, fennel and cumin seed. Turmeric, ground cumin and coriander, ginger, garlic, smoked paprika, cayenne, cinnamon powder and quills, cloves, star anise, whole nutmeg, black peppercorns, garam masala, Chinese five spice, curry powder and sea salt. Loose leaf assam, breakfast, earl grey, jasmine green, peppermint, chamomile and chai, plus coffee roasted here in Liverpool by Crosby Coffee.

Oils, vinegars & pantry

Olive and rapeseed oil poured straight into your own bottle, or ready-bottled in 500ml. Apple cider and balsamic vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, light tahini, smoked chilli harissa and set honey.

Cleaning refills

Plant-based and cruelty-free. Washing-up liquid in citrus, spiced ginger or unfragranced, non-bio laundry liquid, concentrated washing powder, fabric conditioner, antibacterial multi-surface cleaner, glass cleaner, toilet cleaner, hard floor cleaner, hand soap, cleaning vinegar, bicarbonate of soda and dishwasher salt. Dishwasher tablets, rinse aid, stain remover bars and Cheeky Panda paper goods too.

Toiletries & plastic-free

Shampoo, conditioner and body wash on refill in coconut & argan, tea tree & aloe vera and lavender & geranium, plus hand & body lotion, toner, micellar water, facial moisturiser and bath salts. Soap, shampoo, conditioner and shaving bars, toothpaste tablets, powder and jars, mouthwash tablets, floss, interdental sticks, reusable cotton pads and loofahs.

Sweets, snacks & drinks

Vegan pick & mix by weight — fudge, cola bottles, fizzy mix, jelly meerkats, fangs and seasonal shapes. Bombay mix, sweet chilli fava beans, Two Farmers crisps, salt candies, drinking and hot chocolate. Follow @ecorefill_liverpool to see what's just landed.

Order ahead

Short on time? Send us your list and we'll weigh it out during the day, ready for whenever you can get in.

  1. Pick what you want and how much of it, in grams or millilitres.
  2. Email ecorefilluk@gmail.com or text 07547 979864.
  3. Pick it up and pay on any of our open days. Give us a few hours' notice and we'll have it ready.

Bring an empty jar along for next time and we'll fill that instead.

Local & handmade

Not everything here comes out of a dispenser. We keep a shelf of small-batch things made round here — the sort of thing that makes a good gift.

  • Crosby Coffee — roasted here in Liverpool. Ten Street, Colombia and Brazil beans, ground to order.
  • Liverpool Bee Balms — made with wax from local beehives. Lip, hand, body and face balms, a dog paw balm, candles and beeswax polish.
  • Nina's Oil — handmade rose oils for face and body, blended with organic olive or coconut oil. Tea rose, wild rose and damask.
  • Wild Tribe Heroes — children's books about ocean plastic, balloon releases and climate change. £7.99 each.
  • Made by us — hand-drawn greeting and Christmas cards, knotted and fabric earrings, necklaces, hand-knitted merino snoods, tassel keyrings and woollen baubles.
  • Liverpool wildflower seed bombs — seed harvested in the city, £3.
