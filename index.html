Plant based, and made round here

Vegan friendly

Whole foods, plant based cleaning products and plant based toiletries. It's the kind of shop where you don't have to turn the packet over and read the small print — mostly because there isn't a packet.

Local, handmade, small batch

Alongside the refills we keep a shelf of handmade artisan things from makers round here — the sort of small-batch goods that make good gifts. It changes as the makers do, so have a look when you're in.

Bring anything that holds something

1

Bring a clean container

A jar, a tub, a bag, an old bottle — anything you like. Forgot one? We have jars and paper bags on the counter.

2

Weigh, fill, weigh again

Pop it on the scales empty, fill it from the dispenser, and weigh it full. We take the empty weight off.

3

Pay for what you take

Everything is priced per 100g, so a spoonful of spice costs a spoonful. No packaging to throw away.

Here at EcoRefill, our mission is to reduce single use plastic waste and provide a sustainable alternative to everyday products
