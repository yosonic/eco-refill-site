Our story

Here at EcoRefill, our mission is to reduce single use plastic waste and provide a sustainable alternative to everyday products

A small shop with a simple idea

EcoRefill is a bring-your-own-container refill shop on the ground floor of the Toxteth TV building, on Windsor Street in Liverpool 8. We sell the everyday things — nuts, grains, pasta, washing-up liquid, shampoo — loose and by weight, so you take home exactly what you need and nothing you'll throw away.

 

Less in the bin

Every jar refilled is a packet that was never made. Our regulars tell us their kitchen bin fills up half as fast.

 

Only what you need

No minimums, no multipacks. A spoonful to try, a kilo if you're stocking up.

 

Local and friendly

We're a neighbourhood shop. Come in, have a chat, and if there's something you'd like us to stock, tell us.
