How it works

Bring anything that holds something

If you've never used a refill shop, it takes about a minute to get the hang of. Here's the whole thing.

1

Bring a clean container

A jar, a tub, a bag, an old bottle — anything you like, as long as it's clean and dry. Forgot one? We have jars and paper bags on the counter, and a box of donated ones you're welcome to take.

2

Weigh, fill, weigh again

Pop your empty container on the scales and note the weight (or we'll do it for you). Fill it from the dispenser or jar. Weigh it again. We take the empty weight off so you only pay for what's inside.

3

Pay for what you take

Everything is priced per 100g or 100ml and the price is on the dispenser. Card or cash. No packaging goes home with you and nothing goes in the bin.

Containers we love

  • Glass jars with a lid — pasta sauce, jam, coffee, anything
  • Takeaway tubs and old ice-cream tubs
  • Empty washing-up and shampoo bottles (rinsed) for liquids
  • Cotton produce bags for dry goods you'll decant at home
  • Paper bags, if that's all you've got

Good to know

  • Liquids need a container that seals — a lid or a pump
  • Label your jars at home so you remember what's what
  • Bringing a few small jars beats one big one; you'll fill more variety
  • Kids are welcome to do the filling — it's the best bit

Questions people ask on their first visit

Do I have to bring my own container?

No. It's the greenest way to shop with us, but we keep jars and paper bags on the counter if you've come straight from work or just happened past.

Is it more expensive than the supermarket?

Mostly no, and often less, because you're not paying for packaging or a brand. Where something organic costs more, the price is on the dispenser so there are no surprises at the till.

Is everything organic?

Most of the dry goods are, and it's marked on the dispenser where that's the case. Everything is plant-based; the cleaning and toiletry refills are cruelty-free.

Can I buy a tiny amount to try something?

Yes — that's the point. There's no minimum. A tablespoon of a new spice or a handful of a nut you've never tried is perfectly normal.

Do you take card?

Card and cash, including contactless.

Where do I park?

There's on-street parking on Windsor Street and the roads around Toxteth TV. We're also a short walk from the bus stops on Upper Parliament Street and Princes Avenue.
